### Overall Evaluation

After participating in the **First Cloud Journey** program, I feel that this internship was very valuable because the content was not limited to theory but was also connected to hands-on labs, weekly worklogs, and a technical workshop. The program helped me gain a clearer view of AWS Cloud, practical system deployment, and how to document results with evidence for reporting and handover.

### Learning and Working Environment

The program environment was friendly, open, and suitable for interns. The content was organized by week, which made it easier for me to track progress, understand what I needed to learn, and identify what I needed to complete. The combination of AWS service learning, lab practice, blog/worklog writing, and workshop development made the learning process more connected and practical.

I also appreciated the sharing spirit within the team. When issues appeared during implementation or documentation, team members could discuss and agree on how to present content, use evidence, and define the scope clearly to avoid claiming more than what was actually implemented.

### Support from Mentor and Team

The mentor provided strong support throughout the internship, especially in areas that required architectural thinking, report review, and evidence-based writing. Instead of simply giving direct answers, the mentor often guided me to re-check the problem, read more documentation, and understand the root cause before making changes. This approach helped me remember the knowledge longer and become more confident when facing similar issues.

The admin team also provided sufficient information, procedures, and support for the program. As a result, I could focus more on learning, practicing, and completing the report.

### Relevance to My Major

The internship tasks were highly relevant to information technology, especially cloud computing, backend, networking, security, and system monitoring. Services such as IAM, VPC, EC2, S3, SQS, RDS, CloudFront, WAF, CloudWatch, and Security Hub are practical and helped me understand how cloud services work together in a real system.

The **FCAJ Hybrid SOC/AWS** workshop was also very useful because it showed the connection between security and cloud architecture. Instead of configuring services separately, I learned how to describe a system flow that includes event ingestion, asynchronous processing, data storage, monitoring, and alert visualization on a dashboard.

### Learning Opportunities and Skill Development

During the internship, I developed several important skills:

* Reading AWS documentation and selecting relevant information for implementation.
* Deploying, validating, and recording evidence for each service.
* Writing technical reports with a clear structure.
* Analyzing errors and documenting troubleshooting steps.
* Collaborating with teammates on workshop content and report scope.

These skills helped me understand that working with cloud is not only about configuring services in the Console. It also requires understanding architecture, security, cost, monitoring, and how to prove deployment results.

### Most Satisfying Part

What satisfied me the most was having access to a practical learning path. Week by week, I could see my own progress, from learning basic AWS services to writing worklogs, analyzing architecture, and building a workshop for a Hybrid SOC/AWS system. The requirement to document evidence also helped me become more careful and develop the habit of validating before making conclusions.

### Suggestions for Improvement

I hope the program can include more short review sessions at each stage, for example every two or three weeks, so interns can receive earlier feedback on worklogs, blogs, or workshop content. This would help reduce the amount of revision needed near the end of the internship.

In addition, it would be helpful to have more career-sharing sessions about roles such as Cloud Engineer, DevOps Engineer, Security Engineer, or Solutions Architect. These sessions could help interns better understand what to learn after the program and how to build a stronger portfolio.

### Future Expectations

If there is an opportunity, I would like to continue participating in advanced First Cloud Journey activities, especially topics related to real-world cloud architecture, AWS security, DevOps, CI/CD, observability, and production-ready system deployment.

I would also recommend this program to other students who are interested in cloud or security, because it is a suitable environment for learning from the fundamentals, practicing with guidance, and developing professional technical reporting skills.
