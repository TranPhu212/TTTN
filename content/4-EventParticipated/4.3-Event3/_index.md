# Summary Report: “Event Cloud Architect”
### Purpose of the Event
* Create an academic competition for members interested in Cloud Computing
* Assess and reinforce knowledge related to popular AWS certifications:
  * AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02)
  * AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate (SAA-C03)
  * AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Professional (SAP-C02)
* Encourage continuous learning, knowledge sharing, and teamwork
* Provide opportunities for networking and interaction among participating teams within the AWS community

### Competition Format
* The event consisted of a total of 8 participating teams
* Teams were matched in a direct knockout competition format
* Each match included multiple AWS-related multiple-choice questions covering three topics:
  * Cloud Practitioner
  * Solutions Architect Associate (SAA)
  * Solutions Architect Professional (SAP)
* The team with the higher number of correct answers advanced to the next round

### Participation Journey
##### First Match
* My team competed against another team through a series of AWS Cloud multiple-choice questions
* The questions focused on fundamental Cloud Practitioner concepts and several common architectural scenarios from the SAA certification
* Thanks to effective collaboration among team members and our ability to analyze questions quickly, our team secured a victory and advanced to the next round

##### Second Match
* In the following round, the difficulty level of the questions increased significantly
* Many questions required a deeper understanding of architecture design, cost optimization, security, and system scalability on AWS
* Although we actively discussed and selected the most suitable answers, our team was unable to overcome the opposing team and was eliminated after this match

### Key Knowledge Areas Covered
##### AWS Cloud Practitioner
* Cloud deployment models
* Shared Responsibility Model
* Core AWS services
* Concepts related to security, cost management, and governance in the Cloud

##### AWS Solutions Architect Associate (SAA)
* Designing highly available architectures
* Building scalable systems
* Selecting appropriate AWS services based on business requirements
* Optimizing system cost and performance

##### AWS Solutions Architect Professional (SAP)
* Designing large-scale enterprise architectures
* Migration from on-premises environments to the Cloud
* Multi-Account Strategy
* Disaster Recovery and Business Continuity
* Security and Governance in AWS environments

### Key Takeaways
##### Technical Knowledge
* Gained a better understanding of how AWS designs and operates large-scale systems
* Strengthened knowledge of core AWS services such as EC2, S3, RDS, VPC, IAM, and Route 53
* Improved the ability to analyze real-world architectural scenarios

##### Teamwork Skills
* Assigned responsibilities based on each member’s strengths
* Discussed and made decisions under time constraints
* Learned how to listen to and consolidate team opinions to choose the most appropriate answer

##### Certification Exam Skills
* Became familiar with question styles similar to AWS Certification exams
* Practiced interpreting requirements and eliminating distractor answers
* Identified areas that require further study to prepare for future AWS certifications

### Applications to Study and Work
* Continue studying topics within the AWS Cloud Practitioner, SAA, and SAP certification paths
* Apply Cloud architecture design principles to real-world projects
* Improve the ability to select appropriate AWS services for different business scenarios
* Better prepare for future AWS certification exams

### Event Experience
##### Competition Atmosphere
* The event was conducted in a spirit of healthy competition among teams
* Questions ranged from beginner to advanced levels
* Every match was exciting and required a high level of concentration

##### Personal Experience
* This event provided an opportunity to evaluate my current understanding of AWS Cloud concepts
* Winning the first match increased my confidence in the knowledge I had acquired
* Losing in the next round helped me recognize knowledge gaps and areas that need further improvement

##### Networking and Learning
* Had the opportunity to connect with other Cloud Computing enthusiasts
* Observed how other teams analyzed questions and developed answering strategies
* Learned different perspectives and practical experiences from members of the AWS community

##### Lessons Learned
* Having a strong foundation is essential, but it is not sufficient to handle advanced-level questions
* Topics related to system architecture and solution design require more focused study
* Effective teamwork can provide a significant advantage in knowledge-based competitions
* Participating in real-world competitions is an excellent way to assess one's capabilities and identify future learning goals
